What to do in an emergency

  1. Move towards a safer, populated location if possible.
  2. Call 112 if you are facing immediate danger.
  3. Contact someone you trust.
  4. Share your location with a trusted person if safe to do so.
  5. Avoid confronting a threatening person.

SAKHI AI is a student prototype and is not a substitute for emergency services.
